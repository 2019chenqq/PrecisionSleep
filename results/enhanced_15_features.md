# Enhanced: 15 EEG Features

## Added Features

In addition to the five band-power features:

- RMS
- Standard deviation
- Peak-to-peak amplitude
- Zero-crossing rate
- Spectral entropy
- Dominant frequency
- Spectral edge frequency 90%
- Delta/theta ratio
- Alpha/theta ratio
- Sigma/theta ratio
